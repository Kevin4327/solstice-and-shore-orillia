Our Approach

Built for Orillia locals, weekenders, and boaters alike.

Solstice & Shore started with a straightforward goal: pair honest wood-fired cooking with a bright, relaxed room steps from Centennial Park and the Port of Orillia.

Whether you are stopping in after the Saturday Farmers’ Market or hosting a long dinner on the patio, every plate is cooked to order using whole ingredients.
